System Permissions

ASTRA needs permissions to work. When System Settings opens, find ASTRA in the list and enable it.

🔧 If you don't see ASTRA in the list:
  • Close System Settings completely
  • Click the button again to reopen
  • ASTRA should now appear - toggle it ON
  • Close & reopen ASTRA
🎙️

Microphone (Required)

Needed to hear your voice and transcribe it locally. Click to enable → allow when prompted.

⌨️

Accessibility (Required)

Allows ASTRA to automatically paste transcribed text. Click the button → enable ASTRA in the list → restart app.

👁️

Screen Recording (Optional)

Allows ASTRA to see what's on your screen so the AI can provide context-aware responses.
